SD-CWT statement redaction

Operator tooling. Load the unredacted statement from GET /operator/statements/{txid}, choose which {{ chunk_chars }}-character body chunks to keep, and export a copy with the rest withheld. The payload and signature are never touched, so the embedded receipt still verifies.

1 Load

The statement carries its own receipt. Supply the service certificate to check that receipt; without it the disclosures are still hash-matched.

No ledger to hand? Issue a sample locally

Real schema, real chunking — but no receipt, since only the service can issue one.

2 Redact

Select text and withhold it. You can only narrow what is disclosed — chunks already withheld in the loaded statement have no opening to restore, and are marked as locked.

3 Verify

Checks the embedded CCF receipt against the service identity, then hash-matches every attached opening against the signed payload.
